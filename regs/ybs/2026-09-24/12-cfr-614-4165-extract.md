## Relevance to this pack

The source requires Farm Credit banks and direct lender associations to establish
and oversee a YBS program, including program components and annual quantitative
goals. It defines covered credit and services. It does **not** provide the
numeric young-age, beginning-farming-years, or small-sales thresholds used by
this pilot, nor a universal entity-attribution rule. Those fields are therefore
explicitly marked as institution policy in the associated rule pack.

## Reviewed extract

Section 614.4165(a) defines credit to include loans and participation interests
made to farmers, ranchers, and aquatic-product producers or harvesters under
titles I or II of the Act; it defines services to include leases and related
services to the same groups. Section 614.4165(b) requires written policies that
direct affiliated associations to establish a YBS program and requires an annual
FCA report summarizing YBS program operations and achievements. Section
614.4165(d) requires each direct lender association board to establish a sound,
constructive YBS credit-and-services program with specified governance,
credit/service, outreach, and annual quantitative-goal components.
